# Crypto Fund Research Mandate

Status: Incomplete

As of: 21-08-2026

## Approved authority

- Monitor and research crypto markets.
- Hunt for investment and tactical opportunities.
- Produce watchlists, thesis work, scenarios, alerts, and risk reviews.
- Use five specialist research functions under a Chief of Crypto.

## Prohibited authority

- No trade placement or routing.
- No exchange, brokerage, custody, or wallet connection.
- No asset transfer, transaction signature, smart-contract approval, staking, or bridging.
- No handling of credentials, private keys, or seed phrases.

## Missing Capital Principal inputs

The following inputs have not been provided:

- research universe and market-cap or liquidity floor;
- investment and tactical time horizons;
- excluded assets, sectors, instruments, venues, or jurisdictions;
- base currency;
- risk tolerance and maximum acceptable drawdown;
- current exposure context, if portfolio interaction is wanted;
- whether derivatives data is for signals only or may inform hypothetical setups;
- preferred report and alert cadence;
- threshold for an urgent alert.

## Behavior while incomplete

The desk may perform broad, nonpersonalized market monitoring and research. It may rank research priority and identify invalidation conditions. It must not provide allocation sizing, claim portfolio fit, infer risk tolerance, or treat an idea as ready for action.
